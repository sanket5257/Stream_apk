1. Executive summary — the opportunity

StreamForge turns a normal Android phone into a professional TV-style live broadcast studio for YouTube — moving news ticker, channel logo, breaking-news lower-thirds, image/video/web overlays — with no PC and no OBS.

The equivalent professional setup (PC + OBS + capture card + technician) costs ₹40,000–60,000 and needs technical skill. You sell the same result on a device the customer already owns. That price gap is your business.

You already own the hard part. Your invite-code + single-device-binding system is a real licensing / anti-piracy engine. Most apps have to build that before they can charge. You just point it at a payment.

ADV > bare app price because ~50–60% of buyers add branding/setup. Add template-pack re-sales (₹999 × existing base) on top — pure upside.

The repeat-revenue effect (same customers)

Say you reach 100 customers in 18 months. Even with a one-time app, ongoing income:

  • 30% buy 2 template packs/year → 30 × 2 × ₹999 ≈ ₹60,000/yr
  • 20% buy a Pro upgrade later → 20 × ₹4,000 = ₹80,000
  • New branded overlays / event setups on request → ₹50,000+/yr
Takeaway: one-time ≠ one payment. Designed right, each customer is worth ₹6,500 upfront + ₹1,500–3,000/year in add-ons.

7. One-time vs Subscription — the big decision

This is the question that changes your income ceiling. Here’s the honest comparison.

One-time (now)Subscription
Local sales resistanceLow — people love “pay once”Higher — “why pay every month?”
Cash nowHigh upfrontLow upfront, grows over time
Long-term incomeMust keep finding new buyersCompounds — renews automatically
Income ceilingLimited by new sales each monthMuch higher (stacking base)
Best forEasy local closing todayScale & predictable revenue

The money math that proves the difference

One-time: 10 sales/month × ₹6,500 = ₹65,000/month — but only if you keep selling 10 new every month. Stop selling → income stops.

Subscription at ₹299/month/channel — the base stacks:

MonthActive subscribersMonthly recurring (₹299 each)
330₹8,970
670₹20,930
12150₹44,850 / month
24300₹89,700 / month (₹10.7 L/yr, recurring)

Even with some monthly cancellations (churn), the base keeps growing because new subs > cancellations. By month 24 you earn every month what a one-time seller must re-earn from scratch.

Recommended: don’t pick one — do a hybrid.
• Sell the app one-time (₹3,999–8,999) — easy local close, immediate cash.
• Add an optional subscription (₹199–299/mo) for things that justify recurring: cloud template library, new overlay packs every month, priority support, multi-platform, automatic updates.
This way people who hate subscriptions still buy (one-time), and serious channels add the monthly layer — giving you both cash now and compounding income.
If you go subscription, what changes: you must build (1) recurring billing (Razorpay Subscriptions / UPI autopay), (2) auto-expiry in your license check (you already have the device/validation system — add an expiry date), (3) a clear renewal reminder flow. The good news: your existing validation code is 80% of the way there.

8. How to pitch & close

The 10-second hook (WhatsApp / in person)

“Turn your phone into a TV-style live broadcast — moving news ticker, your logo, breaking-news graphics — without a PC or OBS. One-time payment, yours forever. Want a 1-minute demo?”

The demo (your #1 sales weapon)

Record a 60-second vertical video: open app → tap Go Live → camera appears with scrolling ticker + logo + breaking-news lower-third → end card: “No PC. No OBS. One-time ₹___. WhatsApp now.” Drop it in every relevant WhatsApp/Facebook group. This sells while you sleep.

Handling the 3 common objections

They say…You say…
“Too expensive”“A PC + OBS setup is ₹50,000 and needs a technician. This is the same on your phone, one-time.”
“I’ll think about it”“I’ll set it up free and make your channel logo into the overlay — you go live professionally this week.”
“Does it really work?”Send the demo reel + a real customer’s live stream link. Proof closes.
Close with a bundle, not the bare app. “₹8,999 — app + your branded ticker/logo + I set it up and train you.” Higher value, easier yes, more money per sale.

9. Where to find buyers (local)

  • YouTube search your district + language (“[town] news live”) → find small channels → contact via the channel’s About page / WhatsApp.
  • WhatsApp & Facebook groups for videographers, event managers, reporters — post the demo reel. In local India, these groups are the marketplace.
  • Resellers: camera shops, mobile shops, videographers — give ₹500–1,000 per sale. They talk to your buyers daily.
  • Live event demos: stream one local event free with full overlays. Everyone who sees it becomes a lead; the organizer becomes a customer.
  • Temples & community orgs: offer to set up their daily-aarti stream — they tell other temples.

12. 30 / 60 / 90-day action plan

First 30 days — prove & package

  1. Finalize pricing (Basic ₹3,999 / Pro ₹8,999) + add-on menu.
  2. Record the 60-second demo reel.
  3. Set up Razorpay/UPI link + a Google Sheet CRM.
  4. List 30 local news channels / videographers; message them the reel.
  5. Sign your first 3–5 customers at a discount in exchange for using their live stream as public proof.

30–60 days — scale the channel

  1. Recruit 2 resellers (camera shop + videographer) on commission.
  2. Post the demo + customer streams in 10 WhatsApp/FB groups.
  3. Ship live ticker + scenes; update the demo reel to show switching.
  4. Launch the first paid template pack to existing customers.

60–90 days — decide on recurring

  1. Hit a base of 20–30 customers; collect testimonials.
  2. Add the expiry field to licensing and pilot an optional ₹199–299/mo layer (cloud templates + support) to willing channels.
  3. Target 30 active subscribers to start the compounding base while one-time sales continue.
One-line strategy: Sell the app one-time for easy local cash, attach branding + setup to raise each deal, build resellers to multiply reach, and add an optional subscription for recurring income — so you earn now and compound later.
